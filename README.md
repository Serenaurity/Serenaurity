## Featured projects

### Thrive: Rehabilitation Data Analysis · Jan 2026–present

Academic project for the Ministry of Justice's Kamlangjai program. I linked 12 corrections datamart tables with probation data in DuckDB into person-level Parquet tables covering 2M+ people, with a data contract that identifies usable, proxy, and protected features. The work compares occupational-grant recipients with non-recipients using direct standardization and Benjamini–Hochberg correction, with aggregate-only reporting. Classification models are framed as a rule-consistency check: leading features were inputs to the official classification rule, so they are not evidence of an independent predictive signal.

`Python` `SQL` `DuckDB` `Parquet` `scikit-learn`

### CVE-to-ATT&CK Technique Ranking · Sep 2026–present

Independent research project on ranking MITRE ATT&CK techniques for CVE roles. The public repository’s `main` branch contains the earlier W3 foundation; the research results are pinned at [`2a2a6cf` on `work/w8-ablations`](https://github.com/Serenaurity/cve-attack-role-kg/tree/2a2a6cf36ae85ed22a010da9493c6d5c2fd5736a). Reported sealed-test results across 158 queries: hybrid nDCG@5 **0.5581**, text-only LambdaRank **0.5317**, MiniLM **0.2178**, and BM25 **0.1451**. Ablations attribute the hybrid gain to platform (CPE) features rather than graph structure; a randomized graph scored comparably to the real graph.

`Python` `LightGBM LambdaRank` `BM25` `MiniLM` `pandas`

### Student Dropout Risk Prediction · Aug–Dec 2025

Academic research project using 20,919 undergraduate records (2014–2024). I cleaned course-level records, built credit-weighted term GPAs and academic features, and compared logistic regression, random forest, and XGBoost at three checkpoints. The reported XGBoost ROC-AUC increased from **0.866** after Term 1 to **0.992** after Term 3.

`Python` `pandas` `scikit-learn` `XGBoost`
